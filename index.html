Distributed Systems · Rust / Lean 4

MCL4-COE & NCM ↗

ML Architecture · PyTorch / Hugging Face

Consumer-GPU sparse MoE & single-softmax cognitive operators.

A custom language model architecture combining Markov-Chain Local-4 sparse attention, a Causal Encoder-Decoder (CED) MoE backbone, and a Cognitive Operator Ensemble that decomposes reasoning into composable single-softmax heads. Engineered for 12 GB consumer GPUs using NVFP4 2:4 sparsity and CPU-offloaded AdamW master weights in system RAM, with pretrained 2.43B and 1.77B checkpoints published on Hugging Face.

  • PyTorch
  • Sparse MoE
  • NVFP4 2:4
  • CPUOffloadAdamW
  • Safetensors
